# BRAND 2012 Is Gone From NineDots' Website — Here's What Outward 2 Fans Should Know

If you've been digging into NineDots Studio's back catalog lately, you may have hit a dead end. As of late August 2026, the download page for **BRAND 2012** — the studio's very first game and a spiritual pseudo-prequel to Outward — no longer exists on the NineDots website. Visiting the old page now redirects you straight to the **Outward 2** landing page.

This was flagged on [Reddit's r/outwardgame](https://www.reddit.com/r/outwardgame/comments/1vzqi80/brand_2012_avilability/) by a player who had just discovered the game and was looking for a download link. NineDots has not made any public statement about the redirect, so what's going on — and why should Outward 2 players care?

---

## What Is BRAND 2012?

Before Outward, before the survival RPG formula NineDots became known for, there was **BRAND 2012**. Released as a small, experimental title, it was the studio's first publicly available game and laid early groundwork for the themes and tone that would define Outward. The game is widely described by the community as a "pseudo-prequel" — it shares narrative and thematic DNA with the Outward universe, though it predates the franchise proper.

BRAND 2012 was never a mainstream release. It existed primarily as a free download directly from NineDots' own website, making it a piece of gaming history accessible only to those who knew to look. That window now appears to be closed.

NineDots is a small independent studio, and their web presence has always been relatively lean. It's not unusual for small developers to consolidate pages as they move deeper into a major project cycle — but the timing here is notable.

---

## Why the Redirect Matters

The redirect from the BRAND 2012 page to Outward 2 is, at minimum, a housekeeping signal. NineDots appears to be reorganizing their public-facing presence around their upcoming sequel. Whether this means BRAND 2012 is permanently retired from the web, bundled into some future release, or simply deprioritized during the Outward 2 development push is unknown.

**What we can confirm:** As of late August 2026, there is no working official download link for BRAND 2012 on NineDots' website. The page redirects to Outward 2. This was verified by community members, not disputed by NineDots, and not accompanied by any announcement.

**What remains unconfirmed:** Whether NineDots plans to re-release or preserve BRAND 2012 in any form — as a standalone download, a bonus for Outward 2 buyers, or an archival release — is entirely community speculation at this point. No official statement has been made.

## Why Outward 2 Players Should Pay Attention

You might be wondering why any of this matters if you're primarily here for Outward 2. A few reasons:

### 1. Lore and World-Building Context

Outward's world of Aurai didn't emerge from nothing. NineDots has always been a studio with a coherent creative vision, and BRAND 2012 represents the earliest iteration of the ideas that evolved into the Outward universe. For players who want to understand the roots of the setting — the tone, the themes of consequence and survival, the lack of hand-holding — BRAND 2012 offers a window into where NineDots' design philosophy came from.

If Outward 2 expands on Outward's lore or revisits world-building concepts from earlier in the studio's history, knowing BRAND 2012 exists gives you a richer frame of reference.

### 2. Understanding NineDots as a Developer

NineDots is not a typical RPG studio. They've consistently made games that push back against player comfort zones — no traditional fast travel at launch, a stamina and survival system with real teeth, death mechanics that feel meaningful rather than cheap. That philosophy was already present in BRAND 2012.

As hype builds around Outward 2 and new players enter the community, understanding the studio's origins helps set realistic expectations. This isn't a studio that pivots to mainstream accessibility because players ask nicely. That consistency is part of what the core fanbase values.

### 3. The Redirect May Signal a Focused Development Phase

Small studios don't always make deliberate PR moves — sometimes a redirect is just a redirect. But it's worth noting that NineDots pointing every legacy page toward Outward 2 suggests the team is deep in production. Developers often tighten their web presence when they're heads-down on a major release. If you've been hoping for frequent dev updates, this might temper expectations — but it also suggests the studio is working, not drifting.

## Can You Still Play BRAND 2012?

This is the practical question, and right now the answer is: **not easily through official channels**.

The community is currently searching for archived copies or mirrors. As of this writing, no confirmed legitimate archive has been publicly identified in the thread that flagged this change. If you're interested in tracking this down, the r/outwardgame subreddit is the most active place to watch — community members are actively looking.

It's worth noting: BRAND 2012 was originally distributed for free by NineDots, so there's no commercial harm in archiving it, though the legal nuances of hosting another developer's game without explicit permission still apply. Proceed with judgment.

## The Bigger Picture for Outward 2

What this episode really highlights is how much of NineDots' history exists in informal corners of the internet rather than in curated archives. Outward itself took years to find its audience — a cult RPG that rewarded patience and punished shortcuts. As Outward 2 approaches, there's a new wave of players discovering the series for the first time, and many of them don't know BRAND 2012 existed at all.

For a game series built around the idea that the world doesn't revolve around you — that your character is not a Chosen One, that survival requires actual preparation — it's fittingly on-brand that even the studio's own history requires a bit of legwork to uncover.

If NineDots does eventually resurface BRAND 2012 in some form, it would be a meaningful gesture for a community that cares deeply about the series' roots. For now, it's worth keeping an eye on any official announcements, and it's a good reminder to check whether you have any archived files from the original download period.
